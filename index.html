My dearest Stella,

Where do I even begin? Some people come into your life and quietly rearrange everything — they become the soundtrack to your best days, the voice on the other end of the phone at 2am, the person who knows what you mean before you finish your sentence. That's you, Stella. That has always been you.

I think about all the moments we've shared — the ridiculous ones, the serious ones, the ones where we laughed until breathing felt optional — and I feel so incredibly lucky that the universe decided we should find each other. Not everyone gets a friend like you. I know how rare this is.

So on this birthday, I want you to know something, truly: you are one of the most beautiful, brilliant, and kind souls I have ever met. The world is genuinely better with you in it. I am better with you in it.

May this year bring you everything your heart has quietly been wishing for. May you be surprised, celebrated, and loved beyond measure. And may you always, always know how deeply you are cherished.

With all my love, always,
